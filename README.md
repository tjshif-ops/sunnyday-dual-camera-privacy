Privacy Policy for SunnyDay Dual Camera
Effective date: June 16, 2026
SunnyDay Dual Camera ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a camera utility app that lets users take photos and record videos using single-camera or dual-camera modes. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, collects, uses, and shares.
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
Email: tjshif@gmail.com
1. Information We Access or Collect
Camera
The App requests access to your device camera so that you can preview, take photos, and record videos. Camera input is processed on your device for capture, preview, and dual-camera composition.
We do not upload your camera preview, photos, or videos to our servers.
Microphone
The App may request microphone access when video recording with audio is enabled. You can turn off recording audio in the App settings.
Microphone audio is used only to record audio into videos that you choose to capture. We do not upload microphone audio to our servers.
Photos and Videos
Photos and videos created by the App are saved to your device's system media library or gallery. The App may access recently saved media so it can show a thumbnail and open the most recent photo or video from the App.
Your photos and videos remain on your device unless you choose to share, upload, or back them up using other apps or services outside SunnyDay Dual Camera.
Purchases and Subscription Status
The App offers an optional subscription through Google Play Billing. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
The App may store your subscription status locally on your device so it can remove ads, remove watermarks, and apply premium features after purchase.
Advertising Data
The free version of the App may show ads on the Settings screen using Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect or process data such as device identifiers, approximate location inferred by Google, ad interactions, diagnostics, and usage data, depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's policies.
For more information about how Google uses data, please review:
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
2. How We Use Information
We use information accessed by the App to:
Provide camera preview, photo capture, video recording, and dual-camera composition.
Save photos and videos to your device's media library.
Show recent media thumbnails in the App.
Enable or disable audio recording based on your settings and permissions.
Provide optional subscription features, including removal of ads and watermarks.
Show ads to free users through Google AdMob.
Maintain basic App functionality and troubleshoot issues.
3. How Information Is Shared
We do not sell your personal information.
We do not upload your photos, videos, camera preview, or microphone recordings to our servers.
Information may be processed or shared in the following limited cases:
Google AdMob: Free users may see ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may process advertising-related data as described in Google's policies.
Google Play Billing: Subscription purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes payment and subscription information according to Google's policies.
System media library: Photos and videos you create are saved to your device's media library so they can be viewed in your gallery and other media apps.
Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, safety, or security.
4. Data Storage and Retention
Photos and videos are stored on your device in the system media library. You control deletion of those files through your device gallery, file manager, or system settings.
App settings, such as camera mode, picture-in-picture position, audio recording preference, save options, watermark preference, and subscription status, may be stored locally on your device.
We do not operate a cloud account system for the App and do not maintain a server database of your photos, videos, or recordings.
5. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:
Camera: Required for preview, photo capture, and video recording.
Microphone: Used only when recording video with audio enabled.
Media access or media library access, where applicable: Used to save or open photos and videos through the device's system media library.
You can manage App permissions in your Android device settings. If you deny a permission, some features may not work.
6. Children and Age
SunnyDay Dual Camera is not directed to children. The intended audience is users aged 18 and over.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Control ad personalization through your Google account or Android advertising settings, where available.
